# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-xxxx | Abandoned
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: who decided (people or work packages)

## Context
The problem, the constraints, what forces a choice.

## Options considered
| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Option 1 | … | … |
| Option 2 | … | … |

## Decision
What we keep, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences
- ✅ what we gain
- ❌ what we accept to lose (mandatory: a choice presented as perfect is worthless)
- Follow-up: tests, documents, threshold beyond which the decision should be revisited
